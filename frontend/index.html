ENGINEERING & CIRCULAR METHODOLOGY

💡 How ScrapSense AI Works

Architected for real-world electronic component and scrap valuation with zero-shot computer vision.

01

Multimodal Vision AI

Unlike traditional YOLO models bound to 80 generic objects, ScrapSense uses OpenAI's CLIP vision-language transformer to match visual embeddings against electronic and scrap concepts in zero-shot mode.

02

Dynamic Component Valuation

The backend extracts market price bands, material compositions (Gold pins, Silver solder, Copper windings, Aluminium), and computes real-time estimated payouts based on user weight and quantity inputs.

03

Circular Economy Routing

The system guides users away from landfills by recommending licensed smelters, e-waste dismantlers, and upcycling strategies while computing ecological carbon offsets and minerals diverted.
